# Ex.No.-1.2-Design and Draft the given 2D Sketches in modelling software.

## AIM
 To sketch the given drawing with dimensions using fusion 360 tool.
 
## REQUIREMENTS
 1. Autodesk fusion 360
 2. Windows 10
 3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
 4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 
 
 ## PROCEDURE
1.Open Autodesk Fusion 360 and Click on Create Sketch and select a reference plane like XY plane
<img width="296" height="370" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/23ce8d9a-9211-497a-814e-a8addd9d5b55" />

2.Draw the construction lines .At the origin, draw the circles of radii 16 mm, 21 mm, 32 mm and 40 mm with origin as centre point follow this radii respectively.

<img width="410" height="513" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/580d1fe4-c004-48b7-9a8b-ffa8b4e4448c" />

3.Draw a line between the circles of radius 32 mm and 21 mm connecting them and create a circular pattern of six parallel lines of spacing 6mm and trim the unnecessary parts.

4.Draw the circles of radius 15 mm and 20 mm at a point 70 mm from the origin as the centre.

5.Draw the circles of radius 15 mm and 21 mm at a point 60 mm below origin and 50 mm on any one side of the centre construction line.


6.Draw the line and tangent it with the circles of radius 20 mm and 21 mm and trim the unnecessary parts.

<img width="407" height="509" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/80bfbe78-b278-41d5-88a3-9e5b83db8232" />


7.Draw the circle of radius 20 mm and tangent it with the circle of radius 21 mm and circle of radius 40 mm and trim the unnecessary parts.

8.Then mirror the sketch to the other sides.

9.Click finish sketch.
